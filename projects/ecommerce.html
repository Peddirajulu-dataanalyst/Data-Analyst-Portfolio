Ecommerce Revenue Intelligence And Customer Analytics

Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to uncover insights related to revenue performance, customer behavior, profitability, and operational efficiency. The goal is to support data-driven decision-making through SQL analysis and interactive dashboards.

Objectives

  • Analyze sales and profit trends
  • Identify top-performing customers (Pareto 80/20)
  • Perform customer segmentation (RFM)
  • Evaluate customer retention and churn
  • Measure impact of discounts on profitability
  • Analyze returns and their impact on revenue

Technology Used

Python Pandas Numpy Matplotlib Seaborn SQL Power BI Tableau Excel

Project Workflow

  1. Data generation & Collection
  2. Data Cleaning
  3. EDA
  4. Feature Engineering
  5. SQL Analysis,Data modeling & kpi views
  6. Dashboard Creation (Power bi & Tableau)

Business KPIs

  • Total Revenue
  • TOtal Profit
  • Profit Margin
  • Top 20% Revenue percent
  • Return Rate
  • Count of Customers
  • Count of Orders
  • Lost revenue
  • Lost profit
  • Sum of 20% Revenue
